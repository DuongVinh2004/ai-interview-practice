# Privacy and compliance plan

This is an engineering plan, not legal advice. Counsel must validate applicability before launch.

## Principles

Purpose limitation, data minimization, transparency, accuracy, storage limitation, security, user control, and demonstrable accountability apply throughout design. The product is practice-only and forbids automated employment decisions; changing that boundary triggers a new legal, privacy, and AI-risk assessment.

## Data lifecycle

- Present a bilingual privacy notice and explicit consent where required before collecting answers or future recordings.
- Maintain processing inventory, data-flow map, controller/processor roles, subprocessors, transfer basis, retention schedule, and deletion method.
- Support access, correction, export, deletion, consent withdrawal, and complaint workflows with identity verification and audit.
- Production data is excluded from development by default; approved test extracts are minimized and pseudonymized.
- Do not train models on user content without a separate opt-in, documented purpose, retention period, and withdrawal path.

## Legal baselines to assess

Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP are treated as launch baselines from 1 January 2026. GDPR applicability depends on establishment, offering, and monitoring facts. EU AI Act analysis is mandatory before serving EU users; recruitment/candidate-evaluation use is outside this product and can change risk classification.

## Required assessments

Perform a privacy impact assessment before production, voice/recording, new data transfer, new provider, or material profiling. Maintain records of processing, breach response, data-subject request metrics, deletion verification, and subprocessor reviews.
