## Closure controls

- `OPEN` remains the only valid status until its mapped implementation, focused evidence, and required staging validation are complete on a valid candidate/environment.
- `N/A` is permitted only where the row explicitly says why; it never substitutes for a mandatory gate.
- A risk acceptance must name the finding, exact candidate SHA, impact, compensating controls, owner, expiry, monitoring, and approval evidence. No P0/P1 finding may be waived.
- Any source, workflow, Terraform, environment, candidate, or review change triggers the plan's evidence-invalidation matrix.
